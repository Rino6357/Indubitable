---
title: "The Mountain"
date: "2026-05-21"
category: "poem"
---

Life sprouts along cultivated terraces,
On the slopes of an eternal mountain.

Fed on the blood of the royal heiresses,
To decorate THE RULER’s enormous fountain.

Away do the pages burn under his reign,
Ashes of paper telling a tale that ink could not.

Relic of a battle ancient,
Of a time that no longer exists.

The caller of thunder brought before THE KING,
Gongs marking the march of a grand entourage.

Elephants shaking the heavens under his banner,
Ring the bells of merlin, of THE EMPEROR.

Trumpets accompanying his bare footsteps,
An unparalleled procession masking a grim truth.

Once the earth kneeled for the lightning that destroyed and burned order into the land.

Decreed by THE KING who wanted it all, an unforeseen war led under a most powerful general.

Stalemate at THE MOUNTAIN, THE ANGEL found a most formidable human.

On a day of fire and blood did they clash.

On a crimson painted land, THE ANGEL was marked by thunder.

On a day of fire and blood, fell THE EMPEROR.
